When is it clear? The sea of clouds, honestly

Pico do Areeiro is one of the foggiest places on Madeira: it averages 229 fog days a year (INMG 1979, cited in Prada et al. 2009). The north-east trade winds push moist air against the island's east–west mountain barrier, so cloud builds on the north side, usually between about 600–800 m and 1,600 m.

A warm layer above it, the trade-wind temperature inversion, puts a lid on that cloud (mechanism). That is why the peaks at about 1,800 m can stand above a flat “sea of clouds” while Funchal is grey. But the summit is also often inside the cloud, and nobody measures how often the sea of clouds forms at Areeiro, so we don't give odds.

  • Rule of thumb, not a forecast: summit humidity below about 90 % means the station is probably out of the cloud; 90 % or more usually means you're in it.
  • The webcam is the real test. Look before you drive up, and again before you set off.
  • It rains a lot up here: about 2,928 mm a year at Areeiro against about 513 mm in Funchal.

Cold, frost, snow and wind

  • Much colder than Funchal. On the 24 hours we sampled (29–30 September 2026), IPMA measured the summit 8.8–13.8 °C colder than Funchal, most of all around dawn. On inversion days the gap can shrink. The tiles above show the latest pair.
  • Frost and snow happen. The summit dropped to −3.3 °C on 19 March 2026 (DN) and snow whitened the highest peaks on 7 April 2026 (DN).
  • Wind. We flag strong wind from 40 km/h. There is no official IFCN wind limit. The summit wind sensor is often out of action; when it is, we show the reading from IPMA's lower Areeiro station, about 2 km away.
  • IFCN's own trail panels say: “Weather subject to frequent changes, take warm clothing.”

Pico do Areeiro weather: questions

Is there a live webcam at Pico do Areeiro (Arieiro)?

Yes. NetMadeira runs a live camera at the summit that pans between the viewpoint, the start of the PR1 path and the nearby peaks. It is embedded on this page with NetMadeira's own share code, and the same camera is on Windy. It is sometimes offline.

How much colder is Pico do Areeiro than Funchal?

Often around 10–14 °C colder, though it varies with the weather. Over 29–30 September 2026 IPMA measured the summit 8.8–13.8 °C colder than Funchal. The summit has had frost (−3.3 °C on 19 March 2026) and snow (7 April 2026). This page shows the latest measured pair.

Will I see a sea of clouds at sunrise?

Nobody can promise it. The trade-wind inversion often keeps the cloud below the peaks, but Pico do Areeiro itself averages 229 fog days a year. Check the webcam and the summit humidity before you go: below about 90 % the station is probably out of the cloud.

What time is sunrise at Pico do Areeiro?

Between about 06:59 in mid-June and 08:10 in early January (Madeira time), and around 08:00 in early October. This page computes today's time. From the summit, with a clear eastern horizon, the sun shows about 6 minutes earlier.

Is this a weather forecast?

No. The readings are IPMA's measured hourly observations from its Pico do Areeiro station. For a forecast, use IPMA, Windy or a mountain forecast; the links are on this page.
