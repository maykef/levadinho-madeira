SIMplifica won't work from abroad?

Since 2026 the classified PR trails managed by IFCN need a booking through the government's SIMplifica portal (a few, such as PR3, PR3.1, PR4 and PR23, need no booking) — and that portal is the single biggest headache for visitors trying to plan from home.

What actually goes wrong — and the workaround

Why won't the SIMplifica website open from my country? Access

SIMplifica is sometimes unreachable from outside Portugal — it loads the first screen then goes nowhere. Turn off any VPN (a foreign exit node can make it worse), try a different browser, or switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data. If it still won't complete, don't waste an evening on it: it works normally once you're on the island, or an operator can book for you.

SIMplifica won't accept my phone number. What do I do? Registration

The portal frequently rejects non-Portuguese mobile numbers. Choose the email registration path instead of the phone one. Account type is "Pessoa Singular" (individual). Your password needs at least 10 characters using 3 of 4 types (upper, lower, number, symbol) — the "suggest strong password" button saves the hassle.

Payment won't go through Checkout

Pay by credit or debit card (Visa or Mastercard) — that's the route that works for visitors. MB WAY and "ATM reference" are for Portuguese bank accounts, so skip them. Your ticket and QR code arrive by email, not SMS.

Book before you fly — the calm version

  1. Create the account this week, from home, on the SIMplifica booking page — not on hike morning at the trailhead with no signal.
  2. Book the exact date and 30-minute entry slot. Slots have limited places and full ones lock; when PR1 reopened in May 2026, its first weekends sold out the day bookings opened.
  3. Screenshot the QR ticket. You may have to show it on the trail, at control equipment or when IFCN staff ask, and there's often no signal to reload your email.
  4. Everyone in the group goes on the booking — including under-12s (free) and residents (free) — or the whole reservation can be refused.
Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500. Same-day slots are possible if any remain, but a fixed travel date is a bad time to gamble on it.

Can I book a Madeira trail after I arrive on the island? On the island

Yes: same-day and next-day booking is allowed if places remain, and the portal works normally from a local connection. But places are limited and full slots lock, so for a fixed date it is safer to book before you fly.
