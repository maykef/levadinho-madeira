Do you need to book the Vereda do Burro?

No. PR3 is managed by the Câmara Municipal do Funchal, so it is not on the SIMplifica booking portal and there is no IFCN fee (IFCN FAQ 1.7). We found no charge of its own. If your walk joins an IFCN-managed PR trail, that stretch needs a SIMplifica ticket. More in free walks in Madeira.

Where does the Vereda do Burro start and finish?

The Vereda do Burro runs from Pico do Areeiro to Ribeira das Cales - Funchal Ecological Park Welcome Centre. It's a point-to-point walk, so it doesn't finish where you parked — sort your return first (two cars, a pre-booked taxi, or a guided walk with transfers).

It's a long mountain drive from the coast, with no shop or reliable signal at most trailheads. Bring water, warm and waterproof layers, and a torch — the weather turns fast up high.

What if PR3 is closed?

If PR3 shows closed, don't walk it: closures apply to everyone, ticket or not. For open alternatives, check the live board.

Before you go

  • No ticket needed. SIMplifica booking and the IFCN fee apply only to trails managed by IFCN; PR3 is managed by the Câmara Municipal do Funchal (IFCN FAQ 1.7).
  • It doesn't loop back. You finish somewhere else — fix your return before you set off.
  • Check the live badge the morning you go. Mountain trails close fast for weather, rockfall or works.
