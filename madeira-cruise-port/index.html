What is there to do at the Madeira cruise port for a day?

The port is in Funchal itself, so a day ashore is a Funchal day: the Mercado dos Lavradores (closed Sunday), the Old Town, the cable car up to Monte — or a taxi loop to the island's viewpoints. The honest version of both, with times and 2026 prices, is in one day in Madeira; with a second day aboard, two days in Madeira covers the lot.

How do I get from the Madeira cruise port to the town centre?

On foot, along the sea. Ships dock on the Pontinha pier; from the cruise terminal it is a flat walk along the pier and the seafront promenade — about 2.4 km to Praça do Povo and about 3 km to the centre around the cathedral and Avenida Arriaga. Allow 30–40 minutes, a little more or less depending on your berth. Taxis are metered at the official tariff; ask for a quote before you ride.

Road distances from OpenStreetMap. The Mercado dos Lavradores is about 2.6 km from the terminal, just past the cable car station.

How do I get from the cruise port to the cable car?

The cable car's base station is on the seafront by the Old Town, about 2.4 km from the cruise terminal — the same flat walk, 30–35 minutes, or a short taxi ride. It runs daily 08:45–17:45 (closed 25 December); €16 one way, €22 return, ages 0–6 free, 7–14 half price. Up at Monte you have the church, the Monte Palace garden and the toboggans — the full plan is in one day in Madeira.

Is there a map of the Madeira cruise port?

Yes — this one: the Pontinha pier, where the ships dock, and the flat seafront walk past Praça do Povo into the centre, with the Mercado, the cable car base station and the Old Town marked.

Is there a webcam of the Madeira cruise port?

Yes — NetMadeira's camera on the Pontinha pier shows the pier and the ships docked at it, and its bay camera shows the whole of Funchal's seafront with any ship in port.

Webcam: NetMadeira, embedded with its share code. Reload the page for the latest picture; the camera is sometimes offline. Full-size webcam. There is also NetMadeira's whole-bay camera: Baía do Funchal.

Can I do a levada walk from the cruise port?

The walk that fits a port call is not a levada but the mountain taste: the out-and-back from Pico do Areeiro to the Pedra Rija viewpoint on PR1 — 1.2 km each way, €4.50, booked online on SIMplifica (how to book), reached by taxi with an agreed return. A real levada is a half-day or more before the ride there and back; if that is why you came, check the best levada walks and today's status first: all Madeira trails — open or closed today.
