Do you need to book the Caminho Real do Monte?

No. PR3.1 is managed by the Câmara Municipal do Funchal, so it is not on the SIMplifica booking portal and there is no IFCN fee (IFCN FAQ 1.7). We found no charge of its own. If your walk joins an IFCN-managed PR trail, that stretch needs a SIMplifica ticket. More in free walks in Madeira.

Where does the Caminho Real do Monte start and finish?

The Caminho Real do Monte runs from Ribeira das Cales - Funchal Ecological Park Welcome Centre to Monte. It's a point-to-point walk, so it doesn't finish where you parked — sort your return first (two cars, a pre-booked taxi, or a guided walk with transfers).

It's a long mountain drive from the coast, with no shop or reliable signal at most trailheads. Bring water, warm and waterproof layers, and a torch — the weather turns fast up high.

PR3.1 by bus

Getting there. We found no bus to Ribeira das Cales, where the walk starts: you need a car, a taxi or a transfer.

Getting back. The walk ends at Monte, which has a bus back to Funchal. Horários do Funchal line 21, Monte → Funchal (departures from Monte). Monday to Friday: 13:05, 13:40, 14:15, 14:40, 15:05, 15:35, 16:15, 17:30, 17:45, 19:05. Saturdays: 15:55, 18:05. Sundays and holidays: 14:25, 14:50, 16:00, 18:05.

Times as printed on the operators' timetables published on SIGA, the Region's public transport site; times at intermediate stops are approximate. No buses on 25 December. Check before you travel.

What if PR3.1 is closed?

If PR3.1 shows closed, don't walk it: closures apply to everyone, ticket or not. For open alternatives, check the live board.

Before you go

  • No ticket needed. SIMplifica booking and the IFCN fee apply only to trails managed by IFCN; PR3.1 is managed by the Câmara Municipal do Funchal (IFCN FAQ 1.7).
  • It doesn't loop back. You finish somewhere else — fix your return before you set off.
  • Check the live badge the morning you go. Mountain trails close fast for weather, rockfall or works.
