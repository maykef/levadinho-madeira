One day in Madeira: the top 5, and what really fits

A cruise call or a short stop: the five places taxi drivers and locals name first, real distances, 2026 prices — and why you must pick a plan, not do it all.

What are the top 5 things to do in Madeira in one day?

The five places taxi drivers and locals name first: the Cabo Girão skywalk, the Curral das Freiras valley, the old town of Câmara de Lobos, Monte above Funchal, and the Mercado dos Lavradores market. They do not all fit in one day — Cabo Girão and Curral das Freiras lie in different directions and Monte takes half a day — so pick one of the two plans below.

  • Cabo Girão skywalk — the glass platform on a 580 m sea cliff, which Visit Madeira calls the highest promontory in Europe. It is a viewpoint, nothing more: 20–30 minutes is enough. €5 for over-12s (2026 rate; children up to 12 and Madeira residents free), open daily 08:00–20:00.
  • Curral das Freiras — the valley in the island's heart. The Eira do Serrado viewpoint above it is the impressive part; the town itself comes 2.6 km further down.
  • Câmara de Lobos — a stroll through the old fishing town and along the pier. The natural lunch stop of the day.
  • Monte — the cable car up from Funchal's Old Town (€16 one way, €22 return), the church, and the Monte Palace garden (€18, up to 14 free).
  • Mercado dos Lavradores — Funchal's market hall: Monday–Friday 07:00–19:00, Saturday to 14:00, closed on Sunday.

How do I see Curral das Freiras, Cabo Girão and Câmara de Lobos in one day?

By taxi, in one loop of about 56 km (Plan A): Funchal up to the Eira do Serrado viewpoint and down into Curral das Freiras in the morning, across to the Cabo Girão skywalk (20–30 minutes there), then down to Câmara de Lobos for lunch and a stroll through the old town and the pier, and back to Funchal early in the afternoon. With the stops it is about a 6-hour day.

How much does a taxi for a day in Madeira cost?

Taxi drivers quoted us about €160 for a 6-hour day out in October 2026 — an indicative figure, not a tariff. Cabo Girão, Curral das Freiras and the Funchal sights lie in different directions, so the price depends on the exact round trip you want: call a rank, describe your loop, and get a quote before you set off. Official contacts: Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476 (online at taxismadeira.pt) and AITRAM +351 291 765 760.

What can I do in Funchal and Monte in one day?

Plan B stays on foot and on the cable car. Morning at the Mercado dos Lavradores (closed Sunday) and the Old Town; then the cable car up to Monte (daily 08:45–17:45, €16 one way, €22 return; ages 0–6 free, 7–14 half price), the church, and the Monte Palace garden (€18, up to 14 free; 09:00–19:00 April–September, to 18:00 in winter). Come down by cable car — or start the descent with the Carreiros do Monte wicker toboggans (Monday–Saturday 09:00–18:00) and continue by bus or taxi.

The Botanical Garden honestly needs the second day: with the market, Monte and Monte Palace, the day is full. Plans A and B together are a two-day itinerary, not one — it is worked out, with times, in two days in Madeira.

Is one day in Madeira enough?

For one of the two plans above, yes; for the whole top 5, no. One day covers either the viewpoints taxi loop or Funchal and Monte — not both, and not the Botanical Garden. If your ship calls for a single day, pick the plan that fits your hours ashore; with two days you do both — see two days in Madeira.

Should I rent a car for one day in Madeira?

We advise against it for this day. Parking is limited at most of these places — and in central Funchal you would pay for a garage while seeing the town on foot. The taxi loop plus the cable car covers the same ground without the parking hunt. If you are set on driving, go early and have a plan B for parking.

Can I fit a levada or a peak into one day?

One short mountain walk fits a port day: the out-and-back from Pico do Areeiro to the Pedra Rija viewpoint on PR1 — 1.2 km each way on the famous stairway ridge, €4.50, booked online on SIMplifica (see how to book). Check the summit first: Pico do Areeiro weather and webcams.

From a cruise ship

Plan A is sized for a port call: about 6 hours door to door from the terminal. Agree the return time with the driver when you book, and keep a margin of at least an hour before all-aboard. What's open in town today — events, lidos, last buses — is on Funchal today.
