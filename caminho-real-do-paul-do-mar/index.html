Do you need to book the Caminho Real do Paul do Mar?

Yes. PR19 is booking-only: once it shows open, book your €4.50 slot on SIMplifica in advance. Under-12s and residents are free but must still be named on the booking. PR19 is paid on its own separate booking and not included in the multi-day passes — check the live status above before you pay.

Where does the Caminho Real do Paul do Mar start and finish?

The Caminho Real do Paul do Mar runs from Prazeres to Paul do Mar. It's a point-to-point walk, so it doesn't finish where you parked — sort your return first (two cars, a pre-booked taxi, or a guided walk with transfers).

It's a long mountain drive from the coast, with no shop or reliable signal at most trailheads. Bring water, warm and waterproof layers, and a torch — the weather turns fast up high.

PR19 by bus

Getting there. Prazeres, where the walk starts, is a bus stop. Rodoeste line 142, Funchal → Prazeres. Every day: 08:05 → 11:15.

Getting back. The walk ends at Paul do Mar, which has a bus back to Funchal. Rodoeste line 142, Paul do Mar → Funchal. Monday to Friday: 14:15 → 16:55. Rodoeste line 80, Paul do Mar → Funchal. Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: 17:00 → 18:40. On weekdays that leaves about 3 hours between arriving at Prazeres and the bus back.

Times as printed on the operators' timetables published on SIGA, the Region's public transport site; times at intermediate stops are approximate. No buses on 25 December. Check before you travel.

What if PR19 is closed or fully booked?

If IFCN closes PR19 (weather, landslip, works) or you can't get a slot: reschedule via the SIMplifica call centre before your date — you can move date/time freely, and switch trail only if yours was officially closed. For open alternatives, check the live board.

Before you go

  • Booking-only. Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500.
  • It doesn't loop back. You finish somewhere else — fix your return before you set off.
  • Check the live badge the morning you go. Mountain trails close fast for weather, rockfall or works.
