Madeira hiking fees 2026

What each official PR trail costs in 2026, who pays nothing, the 1-, 3- and 7-day combined rates, refunds and fines, plus a calculator for your trip. Set by Portaria 801/2025, as amended by Portaria 48/2026.

Who pays nothing (but still books)

  • Children aged 12 and under, visitors included, with proof of age.
  • Residents of the Autonomous Region of Madeira, with a valid SIMplifica resident card plus citizen card or passport.
  • People with a disability of 60% or more, and one guardian accompanying each of them, with proof.
  • Athletes in events authorised by IFCN.

Exempt people must still be registered: book them on SIMplifica like everyone else (Portaria 48/2026 art. 9; IFCN FAQ 1.4, 2.7–2.9). Landowners along the trails and essential public services are outside the scheme altogether.

Which walks need a ticket?

  • IFCN-managed classified PR trails (PR1–PR1.3, PR2, PR5, PR6 and its branches, PR7 to PR22, PR27, PR28 and the three Porto Santo trails): booking and fee required.
  • PR3 Vereda do Burro, PR3.1 Caminho Real do Monte and PR4 Levada do Barreiro (managed by the Funchal council) and PR23 Levada da Azenha (Caniço parish) are classified but not managed by IFCN: they are not on SIMplifica and carry no IFCN fee.
  • Non-classified levadas and paths need no booking and no fee. IFCN doesn't maintain or recommend them.

Time slots, refunds and rescheduling

  • Slots: you book a 30-minute entry window. IFCN allows about 15 minutes' tolerance either side, a little more if you are walking trails in sequence. It is an allowance, not a right.
  • Booking: online only, on SIMplifica. Same-day and next-day booking work while slots remain. Nothing is sold at the trailhead or at IFCN offices.
  • Refund or new date only if IFCN cancels (an official closure or restriction on your date). Email helpdesk.ifcn@madeira.gov.pt within 10 working days of the booked date and ask for a new date within a year on the same trail, or a full refund by bank transfer.
  • Everything else loses the fee: rain, illness, a cancelled flight, a missed slot or a wrong date. The SIMplifica contact centre can't change or refund bookings.

The fine for walking without a ticket

Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (contraordenação) under Portaria 801/2025, art. 10, and Decreto Legislativo Regional 24/2022/M, art. 13. The law sets fines for individuals of €250 to €2,500. Rangers may also ask you to leave the trail. Entering a closed trail carries the same fine range.

What changed from 2025

The 2026 rates come from Portaria 801/2025, which revoked the earlier Portaria 556/2024. Under the old rules the standard fee was reported as €3 per trail; since 1 January 2026 it is €4.50, and booking a time slot is mandatory.

Questions people ask

How much does hiking in Madeira cost in 2026?

€4.50 per person for each classified PR trail managed by IFCN. PR1 costs €4.50 from Pico do Areeiro to the Pedra Rija viewpoint and back, or €10.50 for the full route to Pico Ruivo. Children 12 and under, Madeira residents and people with a disability of 60% or more pay nothing but must still book on SIMplifica.

Are there day or multi-day hiking passes in Madeira?

There are combined rates, not passes: €9 for two or more trails in one day, €22.50 for two or more in 3 days and €52.50 for two or more in 7 days. PR1 is never included. Put all your trails in one SIMplifica booking and the cheapest combination is applied automatically; every trail still needs its own time slot.

Is the fee cheaper with a tour operator?

Yes, if the operator has a protocol with IFCN: €3 per trail, €7 for the full PR1, and €6 / €15 / €35 for the day, 3-day and 7-day rates. Operators without a protocol pay the normal rates.

Can I get a refund if it rains or I can't go?

No. The fee is refunded or moved to a new date only when IFCN officially closes or restricts the trail on your booked date. Email helpdesk.ifcn@madeira.gov.pt within 10 working days to ask for a new date within a year or a refund. Rain, illness or a cancelled flight don't qualify.

What is the fine for hiking without a ticket in Madeira?

Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; Decreto Legislativo Regional 24/2022/M art. 13). The law sets fines for individuals of €250 to €2,500.

Do all levadas need a ticket?

No. Only classified PR trails managed by IFCN need a booking and fee. PR3, PR3.1, PR4 and PR23 are managed by the Funchal council or the Caniço parish and are not on SIMplifica. Non-classified levadas need no booking and no fee.
