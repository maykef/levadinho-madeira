Questions

Which Madeira trails are open today?

This page shows the open, restricted or closed status of every classified PR walking trail from the official IFCN trail-warnings list, with IFCN's own “updated” date (IFCN updates the list when conditions change, not daily). Restricted and closed trails are listed first.

Do I still need to book a Madeira trail if it is open?

Yes, on the classified PR trails managed by IFCN: book and pay in advance on the SIMplifica portal — €4.50 per trail, or €10.50 for PR1 — even when the trail is open. Children under 12 are free but must be on the reservation. A few classified trails (PR3, PR3.1, PR4, PR23) are not on SIMplifica and need no booking.

Where can I check if Madeira's trails are open today?

On the official IFCN trail-warnings list, which is the source for this board. This page shows the same list trail by trail, with IFCN's own “updated” date, and each trail has its own page with a live status card.

Why I built this

In 2026 Madeira put the classified trails managed by IFCN behind paid, timed booking on SIMplifica (a few, such as PR3, PR3.1, PR4 and PR23, need no booking) — and the fees, rules and closures keep changing. Too many visitors turn up to a closed trail or overpay because the guide they read was months out of date. So this site checks the official IFCN trail-warnings list daily and shows it with IFCN's own “updated” date, next to the fees and rules that apply today.
