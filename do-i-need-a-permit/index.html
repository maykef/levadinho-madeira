Do I need a permit for levada walks in Madeira?

Everyone calls it a permit. Officially it's a timed booking (reserva) plus a fee (taxa), paid on the SIMplifica portal. Whether you need one depends on which kind of walk it is.

The short answer: you need a paid SIMplifica booking only for the classified PR trails managed by IFCN, the Region's forest and nature authority. That covers almost all the famous walks: PR1, PR6 25 Fontes, PR8 São Lourenço, PR9 Caldeirão Verde and more. Classified trails run by other bodies (PR3, PR3.1, PR4, PR23) and non-classified levadas need no booking and no IFCN fee.

Three kinds of walk, three answers

1. IFCN classified PR trails Book + pay

35 trails on Madeira and 3 on Porto Santo. Before you walk, book a 30-minute entry slot and pay on SIMplifica: €4.50 per trail, €10.50 for the full PR1 (or €4.50 for Areeiro–Pedra Rija only). Carry the digital ticket; IFCN staff can ask for it.

2. Classified trails run by someone else No IFCN fee

PR3 Vereda do Burro, PR3.1 Caminho Real do Monte and PR4 Levada do Barreiro are managed by the Câmara Municipal do Funchal; PR23 Levada da Azenha by the Junta de Freguesia do Caniço. IFCN's own list names these bodies as managers, and its FAQ (1.7) says a classified trail managed by another entity "is not available for booking on the Portal". They are not part of IFCN's booking system, so there is no IFCN fee.

3. Non-classified levadas and paths Free

Most of Madeira's levada network is not classified, for example Levada dos Tornos, Levada do Caniçal or Levada do Norte. There is nothing to book and nothing to pay (IFCN FAQ 1.7). But they are not classified, so they are not maintained or recommended by IFCN (FAQ 6.2): you walk them entirely at your own risk.

What still applies

  • Exempt people still book. Children 12 and under, Madeira residents, and people with a disability of 60% or more (plus one guardian) pay nothing, but they must be on the SIMplifica booking.
  • Online only. There are no tickets at the trailhead or at IFCN offices. Same-day booking works if slots are left.
  • No guide required. You can walk every PR trail on your own (IFCN FAQ 6.4).
  • Closures apply to everyone. A booking doesn't open a closed trail, and a free levada can still be closed or dangerous. Check the live trail board: its status comes from the official IFCN warnings list.
Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500. Entering a closed trail is an offence under the same article.

Questions people ask

Do I need a permit to walk the levadas in Madeira?

Only on the classified PR trails managed by IFCN. For those you must book a timed entry slot and pay the fee on SIMplifica before you walk (€4.50 per trail, €10.50 for the full PR1). Classified trails run by other bodies (PR3, PR3.1, PR4, PR23) and non-classified levadas need no booking and no IFCN fee.

Is it really a permit?

Not officially. It is a booking (reserva) of a 30-minute entry slot plus a fee (taxa), paid online on SIMplifica. The ticket is personal and valid for your date and slot. You don't need a guide.

Which levada walks are free in Madeira?

PR3 Vereda do Burro, PR3.1 Caminho Real do Monte, PR4 Levada do Barreiro and PR23 Levada da Azenha are classified but managed by the Funchal council or the Caniço parish, so they aren't on SIMplifica and carry no IFCN fee. Non-classified levadas such as Levada dos Tornos need no booking either, but IFCN does not maintain or recommend them.

My children are under 12. Do they need a booking?

Yes. Children 12 and under, residents and people with a disability of 60% or more don't pay, but they must still be included in the SIMplifica booking.

What happens if I walk a PR trail without a ticket?

Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13). The law sets fines for individuals of €250 to €2,500, and rangers may ask you to leave the trail.

Why can't I find a trail on SIMplifica?

According to IFCN, a trail is missing from the portal when it is not classified, when it is classified but managed by another body (PR3, PR3.1, PR4, PR23), or when it is closed.
