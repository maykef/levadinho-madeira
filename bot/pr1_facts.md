## The trail
- PR1 Vereda do Areeiro: Pico do Areeiro (1,818 m) → Pico Ruivo (1,862 m, the highest
  point on Madeira), across the Central Mountain Massif. It passes Pico das Torres
  (1,851 m), Madeira's second-highest peak.
- Official figures: 6.1 km to Pico Ruivo, about 3 hours, moderate difficulty, altitude
  1,491–1,857 m. Starts at the Pico do Areeiro viewpoint.
- The route in use is the west variant, through the tunnels. The east variant over Pico das
  Torres is listed by IFCN as closed.
- What you walk through:
  - the famous "Stairway to Heaven" ridge near the start;
  - tunnels dug into the volcanic tuff, once used to shelter cattle and shepherds (bring a headlamp);
  - a steep staircase carved in the rock around Pico das Torres;
  - the "Homem em Pé" (Standing Man) rock formations;
  - thousands of stone and metal steps;
  - narrow ridges with big drops on both sides, with handrails on some sections.
- The hardest part comes last: the climb up to the Pico Ruivo shelter (Casa de Abrigo do
  Pico Ruivo). The shelter sits just below the summit and has basic facilities.

## Closures and reopening (official, IFCN)
- The August 2024 wildfire (from 14 Aug 2024) and the rockfall it caused closed PR1. The
  Areeiro–Pedra Rija section (1.2 km) reopened on 4 September 2024; the rest stayed closed.
- The full PR1 reopened on **1 May 2026**, at first Fridays to Sundays only. The one-way
  section has been open **every day since 26 June 2026**. (Not "27 April": that was the
  MIUT race; bookings opened on 29 April.)
- Scheduled works: IFCN closed the Pedra Rija → Pico Ruivo section on **14–17 and 21–22
  September 2026**. Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija stayed open both ways on those days.
- Whether the route is open now: only LIVE STATUS says so.

## One-way rule (since the reopening in May 2026)
- The full route is ONE-WAY beyond Pedra Rija: Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo only. You may
  not walk back the same way, and you may not start from Pico Ruivo.
- Two ways to do it:
  1. **Partial:** Pico do Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija viewpoint (1.2 km + 1.2 km), there and back
     along the same path. This covers the Stairway to Heaven section.
  2. **Full:** Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo (6.1 km). One-way from Pedra Rija onwards. The
     full route starts at Pico do Areeiro from the first slot of the day, not at the Pedra
     Rija gate (IFCN FAQ 5.2).
- After Pico Ruivo you leave by PR1.2 down to Achada do Teixeira (Santana), about 2.8 km and
  roughly 45–60 min, or by PR1.1. Allow about 4 hours for the whole walk, more with stops.

## Getting there and back
- IFCN: "Hikers are responsible for their own transport logistics, and IFCN is not
  responsible for the return to Pico do Areeiro." Plan the return before you start; phone
  signal is patchy.
- **Public bus Funchal ↔ Pico do Areeiro** (operator Horários do Funchal, service "Pico do
  Areeiro", https://www.horariosdofunchal.pt/index.php/viajar/pico-do-areeiro, checked 30 Sep 2026):
  - Runs every day, including weekends and holidays, except 25 December.
  - Up: leaves the Teleférico terminus stop in Funchal (Teleférico-Término 4A) at **06:00** and
    **13:30**, and reaches Pico do Areeiro (roundabout by the Radar) at **06:45** and **14:15**.
  - Down: leaves Pico do Areeiro at **12:15** and **19:00** (the 19:00 is **18:00** in the winter
    timetable, from 25 Oct 2026 to 27 Mar 2027).
  - Fare **€3.00 per trip**, paid on board. 31 seats.
  - Seat reservation by phone only, **291 705 555**, from 7 to 2 working days before the trip
    (Mon–Thu 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–18:00, Fri 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–17:00). With a reservation,
    be at the stop 15 min before departure.
  - It fits the Pedra Rija out-and-back (up 06:45, down 12:15), or getting TO the start of the
    full walk without a car.
- **No bus serves Achada do Teixeira**, where the full walk ends. The public bus cannot bring
  you back from a full traverse.
- Ways back from Achada do Teixeira:
  - A pre-booked hiker transfer: drop-off at Areeiro, pick-up at Achada do Teixeira. Best for most people.
  - A guided tour with transport from an IFCN protocol operator.
  - A taxi, arranged in advance (taxis don't wait at the car park). **Táxis Santana: +351 291 572 540**
    (the number on IFCN's trailhead panels). Reported prices, not official: about €70–90 Achada do
    Teixeira → Funchal; say "ask for a quote".
  - Two cars, one left at each end. Only sensible for groups.
- Emergency: 112.

## Fees and booking (2026, Portaria 801/2025 as amended by Portaria 48/2026)
- Booking is mandatory, online only, on SIMplifica:
  https://simplifica.madeira.gov.pt/services/78-82-259 — no cash, no kiosk. Same-day and
  next-day booking are allowed if there are places.
- Full PR1: **€10.50** per person (covers PR1.1, PR1.2 and PR1.3 at no extra fee).
  Areeiro–Pedra Rija only: **€4.50**.
- Booked through an economic operator with an IFCN protocol: **€7** for the full PR1 (€3 for
  Pedra Rija only, €3 for other trails). Only the operator can book at that rate.
- Free, but must still be on the booking: children **12 and under**, Madeira residents, and
  people with a certified disability of 60% or more (plus one accompanying guardian).
- Multi-trail rates: **€9** for 2+ trails in one day, **€22.50** for 3 days, **€52.50** for 7
  days. They are not a pass: every trail still needs its own booking; SIMplifica applies the
  cheapest combination when they're in one booking. **PR1 is never included** in them
  (not even the Pedra Rija ticket): it is always a separate €10.50 / €4.50.
- Entry is in 30-minute time slots, with about 15 minutes' tolerance either side. The earliest
  are 07:00 and 07:30 (added in June 2026). Morning slots sell out first, often days ahead
  in July–August.
- **Fine:** walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an
  administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; Decreto Legislativo Regional 24/2022/M
  art. 13). The law sets fines for individuals of **€250 to €2,500**. Entering a closed trail
  carries the same range. Never say "up to €250". Rangers check the QR ticket, so screenshot
  it because there's often no signal.
- SIMplifica tips for visitors:
  - Register with email if it rejects a foreign phone number. Account type is "Pessoa Singular".
  - Pay by Visa or Mastercard.
  - The ticket arrives by email.
  - Turn off any VPN if the site won't load.
- SIMplifica Contact Center (portal problems): +351 800 29 90 90, WhatsApp +351 926 155 465,
  working days 9:00–18:00. It cannot change or refund bookings.

## Sold out?
- Try weekday mid-morning slots (09:00–10:30).
- Re-check SIMplifica the evening before, because cancellations get released.
- Book through an IFCN protocol operator; they hold their own allocation.
- Or reach Pico Ruivo from the other side on PR1.2 from Achada do Teixeira: about 45–60
  min each way, €4.50, its own booking, usually more availability.

## Refunds and closures (Portaria 48/2026 art. 11; IFCN FAQ)
- Only if IFCN cancels (an official closure or restriction on your booked date) can you ask,
  within 10 working days of the booked date, for either a new date and slot within one year
  (same trail, if there are places) or a full refund.
- Anything else (rain, illness, a cancelled flight, a missed slot) loses the whole amount.
  A weather warning does not close trails automatically; only an official closure counts.
- How to ask: email **helpdesk.ifcn@madeira.gov.pt** with the tickets and invoice (plus the new
  date and slot you want, or for a refund a bank document with IBAN and SWIFT/BIC, the account
  holder's name and your address). IFCN: +351 291 145 590, working days 09:00–12:30 and 14:00–17:30.
- Good alternatives if PR1 is closed:
  - PR1.2 Vereda do Pico Ruivo: the summit without the ridge.
  - PR11 Vereda dos Balcões: 3 km there and back, nearly flat, valley views, good on foggy days.
  - PR9 Levada do Caldeirão Verde: tunnels, waterfalls, bring a headlamp.
  Only suggest ones the live status shows as OPEN. A booking moves to another trail only if
  IFCN agrees, e.g. during a long closure.

## Sunrise
- Computed sunrise at Pico do Areeiro (Portugal time, about ±2 min; ~6 min earlier with a
  clear horizon): about 08:00 early October, 08:12 mid-October, 07:25 early November (winter
  time), 08:10 in January, 07:20 early May, 06:59 mid-June (the earliest), 07:21 early August,
  07:42 early September.
- The public bus reaches Pico do Areeiro at 06:45 every day, before sunrise all year (tight
  margin, ~8–15 min, from late May to mid-July). The stop is the Radar roundabout; allow a few
  minutes' walk to the viewpoint.
- In autumn and winter the 07:00 slot puts you on the ridge in the dark, so bring a headlamp.
- Parking is the bottleneck. Arrive at least an hour before sunrise. The lots fill, and
  police sometimes close the access road. Roadside parking on the ER103 is prohibited.
- Sunrise hiker transfers pick up in Funchal/Caniço around 05:00–06:30. Shared transfers cost
  about €40–49 per person (Sept 2026, sellers' prices); the trail ticket is usually extra.
- About 35–50 min drive from Funchal.
- The Areeiro café opens 09:00, so it is closed at sunrise.

## Weather and safety
- It is often 10–15 °C colder than Funchal, and can be single digits before dawn even in
  August. Fog can sit on the summit while the coast is sunny. Use the mountain forecast.
- The trail may close for strong wind, heavy rain, fog, landslide risk or other adverse weather.
- Bring:
  - a headlamp (tunnels, dawn);
  - windproof and warm layers;
  - proper hiking shoes;
  - 1.5–2 L of water;
  - sun protection;
  - trekking poles (useful on the steps).
- There are sheer drops on narrow ridges, so it's not for people with serious vertigo.
- Best months are May–June and September–October. Mornings until about 09:00–10:00 give
  the best chance of views above the clouds.
- Trail users are personally responsible for any damage or injury (DLR 24/2022/M art. 11).

## Not confirmed — do not state as fact
- Whether you can watch sunrise at the Pico do Areeiro viewpoint without a ticket. The fee
  applies to entering the trail and the viewpoint is by the car park, but no official source
  says so. Say: "the viewpoint by the car park is outside the trail; walking onto PR1 needs a
  ticket — this isn't officially confirmed".
- Parking at Pico do Areeiro: about €4/hour at the upper car park (first 15 min free) is
  reported by two sources; €2/hour at the lower car park is only an announcement. Say "about
  €4/hour at the top car park, check on arrival". Any daily cap is unverified.
- Taxi prices: only reports, no official table. Always "ask for a quote".
- Whether the Santana taxi line is staffed at any given time.
- Exact number of tunnels and the daily capacity.
- The Pico Ruivo shelter and Achada do Teixeira café opening hours (a single, undated report:
  shelter 09:00–17:00, café 09:30–19:00).
