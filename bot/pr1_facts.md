# PR1 Vereda do Areeiro — knowledge for the Levadinho trial bot

Scope: the trial bot covers PR1 only. Today's open/closed status and the official
note are NOT in this file — they come live from status.json at answer time and always
win over anything written here. Compiled 2026-09-26 from the sources listed at the end.

## The trail
- PR1 Vereda do Areeiro: Pico do Areeiro (1,818 m) → Pico Ruivo (1,862 m, the highest
  point on Madeira), across the Central Mountain Massif. It passes Pico das Torres
  (1,851 m), Madeira's second-highest peak.
- Official figures: 6.1 km to Pico Ruivo, about 3 hours, moderate difficulty, altitude
  1,491–1,857 m. Starts at the Pico do Areeiro viewpoint.
- What you walk through:
  - the famous "Stairway to Heaven" ridge near the start;
  - tunnels dug into the volcanic tuff, once used to shelter cattle and shepherds (bring a headlamp);
  - a steep staircase carved in the rock around Pico das Torres;
  - the "Homem em Pé" (Standing Man) rock formations;
  - thousands of stone and metal steps;
  - narrow ridges with big drops on both sides, with handrails on some sections.
- The hardest part comes last: the climb up to the Pico Ruivo shelter (Casa de Abrigo do
  Pico Ruivo). The shelter sits just below the summit and has basic facilities.

## One-way rule (since the reopening on 27 April 2026)
- PR1 reopened on 27 April 2026, after about 20 months of reconstruction following the
  August 2024 wildfire.
- The full route is ONE-WAY: Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo only. You may not walk back the
  same way, and you may not start from Pico Ruivo.
- Two ways to do it, per the official page:
  1. **Partial:** Pico do Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija viewpoint (1.2 km + 1.2 km), there and back
     along the same path. This covers the Stairway to Heaven section.
  2. **Full:** Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo (6.1 km). One-way from Pedra Rija onwards.
- After Pico Ruivo you walk down to Achada do Teixeira (Santana) on the PR1.2 path,
  about 2.8 km and roughly 45–60 min. Allow about 4 hours for the whole walk, more with stops.

## Getting back from Achada do Teixeira (the big catch)
- There is NO public bus at Achada do Teixeira or at Pico do Areeiro, and phone signal is
  patchy. Plan the return before you start.
- Options:
  - A pre-booked hiker transfer: drop-off at Areeiro, pick-up at Achada do Teixeira. Best for most people.
  - A guided tour with transport from an IFCN protocol operator.
  - A taxi from Santana, arranged in advance. Taxis don't wait at the car park. Pico do
    Areeiro ↔ Funchal area runs about €70.
  - Two cars, one left at each end. Only sensible for groups.
  - The bus: get down to Santana first, then take a slow, infrequent bus to Funchal. A last resort.

## Fees and booking (2026)
- Booking is mandatory, online only, on SIMplifica:
  https://simplifica.madeira.gov.pt/services/78-82-259 — no cash, no kiosk.
- Full traverse: €10.50 per person. Partial Areeiro–Pedra Rija section: €4.50.
- Booked through an IFCN protocol operator (licensed guide): €7 on PR1.
- Free, but must still be on the booking: children 12 and under, Madeira residents, and people with a certified disability of 60% or more.
- PR1 is NOT included in the 1/3/7-day multi-trail passes. It is always a separate booking.
- Entry is in 30-minute time slots. The earliest are 07:00–07:30 and 07:30–08:00 (added
  in June 2026, 70 places each). Morning slots sell out first, often days ahead in July–August.
- Hiking without a valid ticket is an infraction, with fines reported up to €250. Rangers
  check tickets (QR code), so screenshot it because there's often no signal.
- SIMplifica tips for visitors:
  - Register with email if it rejects a foreign phone number. Account type is "Pessoa Singular".
  - Pay by Visa or Mastercard.
  - The ticket arrives by email.
  - Turn off any VPN if the site won't load.

## Sold out?
- Try weekday mid-morning slots (09:00–10:30).
- Re-check SIMplifica the evening before, because cancellations get released.
- Book through an IFCN protocol operator; they hold their own allocation.
- Or reach Pico Ruivo from the other side on PR1.2 from Achada do Teixeira: about 45–60
  min each way, €4.50, its own booking, usually more availability.

## If the trail is officially closed
- Contact the SIMplifica call centre before your date (not IFCN).
- You can move to another date or time on the same trail freely.
- You can switch to a different trail ONLY if yours was officially closed.
- Good alternatives:
  - PR1.2 Vereda do Pico Ruivo: the summit without the ridge.
  - PR11 Vereda dos Balcões: 3 km there and back, nearly flat, valley views, good on foggy days.
  - PR9 Levada do Caldeirão Verde: tunnels, waterfalls, bring a headlamp.
  Only suggest ones the live status shows as OPEN.

## Sunrise
- Watching sunrise from the Pico do Areeiro viewpoint is free. No booking is needed; the
  ticket only covers the trail beyond the checkpoint.
- Sunrise is about 06:50 in midsummer and 08:05 in midwinter. In late autumn and winter,
  the 07:00 slot puts you on the ridge in the dark, so bring a headlamp.
- Parking is the bottleneck. Arrive at least an hour before sunrise. The lots fill, and
  police sometimes close the access road. Roadside parking on the ER103 is prohibited.
- Sunrise transfer minibuses pick up in Funchal/Caniço around 05:00–05:30. The trail
  ticket is usually not included.
- About 35–50 min drive from Funchal.

## Weather and safety
- It is often 10–15 °C colder than Funchal, and can be single digits before dawn even in
  August. Fog can sit on the summit while the coast is sunny. Use the mountain forecast.
- The trail may close for strong wind, heavy rain, fog, landslide risk or other adverse weather.
- Bring:
  - a headlamp (tunnels, dawn);
  - windproof and warm layers;
  - proper hiking shoes;
  - 1.5–2 L of water;
  - sun protection;
  - trekking poles (useful on the steps).
- There are sheer drops on narrow ridges, so it's not for people with serious vertigo.
- Best months are May–June and September–October. Mornings until about 09:00–10:00 give
  the best chance of views above the clouds.
