Used for "how do I get from A to B" questions. Start from where the visitor is staying ("Staying in: …").

Visit Madeira (the official tourism site, https://www.visitmadeira.com/pt/meios-de-transporte/taxis/) lists two taxi
contacts for the island: AITRAM +351 291 765 760 and Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476 (the Funchal number above), and

No email was found for Táxis Santana or the other ranks: give the phone number only.

Taxi prices: there is no official fare table. Always say "ask for a quote when you book".

## Transfers

Private hiker-transfer companies and many hotels arrange transfers (for example to Pico do Areeiro for sunrise, or a
pick-up at Achada do Teixeira after PR1). Levadinho does not recommend, name or book any company. Suggest asking the
hotel reception, or booking a taxi from the list above for a set time.

## Buses

Bus times come only from the bus details on our pages (the "Levadas by bus" guide, the PR1 sunrise-transport guide,
each trail's "By bus" section). If those details don't show a bus from the visitor's town, say there is no bus in our
data from there and give the taxi option instead. Never make up a route, stop or time.
