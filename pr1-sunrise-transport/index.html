Getting to Pico do Areeiro for sunrise: bus, transfer, taxi or car

The cheapest way to Pico do Areeiro for sunrise is Horários do Funchal's “Pico do Areeiro” bus: €3, leaving the Teleférico stop in Funchal at 06:00 and arriving at 06:45, before sunrise every day of the year (it runs daily except 25 December). Otherwise take a shared or private transfer (easiest booked through your hotel reception), a taxi, or drive and park at the upper car park (€4/hour). For the full PR1 traverse you also need a lift back from Achada do Teixeira, which no bus serves.

PR1 the public bus in detail

Horários do Funchal runs a named service, “Pico do Areeiro”, every day except 25 December. The summer timetable runs until 24 October 2026; the winter timetable from 25 October 2026 to 27 March 2027.

  • Up: 06:00 and 13:30 from the Teleférico terminus stop (4A) in Funchal, via Monte, Terreiro da Luta and Poiso, arriving at the Radar roundabout at Pico do Areeiro at 06:45 and 14:15.
  • Down: 12:15 and 19:00 from the Radar roundabout (18:00 instead of 19:00 in winter), back at the Teleférico at 13:00 and 19:45.
  • Fare: €3.00 per trip, bought on board. No other transport tickets are accepted. 31 seats.
  • Seat reservations by phone only, 7 to 2 working days before the trip: 291 705 555 (Mon–Thu 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–18:00, Fri 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–17:00). With a reservation, be at the stop 15 minutes before departure.
  • Summit shuttle: €1.20 per trip, every 15 minutes (06:40–12:15 and 14:10–19:00, 18:00 in winter), between the CAM Florestal das Serras de Santo António, the Pico do Areeiro car park and the Radar roundabout. It leaves with at least 4 passengers.
Sunrise margin: the 06:45 arrival beats sunrise every day of the year, but in June and early July only by about 8–15 minutes (table below). The stop is the Radar roundabout, a short walk from the viewpoint. The bus doesn't serve Achada do Teixeira, so it suits sunrise only, an out-and-back to Pedra Rija (€4.50 ticket, back on the 12:15), or the way up if a transfer or taxi collects you at the end.

Shared and private transfers

The easiest way to book a transfer is through your hotel reception: tell them the date and the pick-up time you need.

Several companies run shared vans that drop hikers at Pico do Areeiro before sunrise and collect them at Achada do Teixeira after the traverse. Prices vary, so ask for a quote. This is what their own pages say:

  • Beyond Madeira: shared, up to 9 people, pay on the day, free cancellation up to 48 h; collects at Achada do Teixeira. The page is unclear about whether the trail ticket is included, so ask.
  • Doit Madeira: a PR1 + PR1.2 sunrise transfer; it can also sell you the PR1 ticket at €7.
  • GetToHikes: sunrise transfer with pick-ups at Ajuda, Lido and central Funchal; no price in the page text.
  • Private: madeira.best offers a sunrise round trip to Areeiro by car (up to 3) or minivan (4–8), or a drop at Areeiro with collection at Achada do Teixeira, with hotel pick-up about 1 h 30 before sunrise.

All four companies are listed in Visit Madeira's official directories (travel agencies, tourist entertainment companies).

Why some quote €7 for the ticket and others €10.50: IFCN protocol operators pay the operator rate for PR1, €7, set by Portaria 48/2026. On your own it is €10.50 on SIMplifica. See hiking fees 2026.

Taxi or your own car

  • Taxi: taxis charge by the meter at the official regulated tariff, set by the Region's transport authority (IMT-RAM) with the taxi associations and in force since 1 January 2026. There is no fixed price per route; nights, weekends and holidays cost more, so ask for a quote when you book. Taxis don't wait at Achada do Teixeira: agree a pick-up time. Call the rank nearest the pick-up (Táxis Santana +351 291 572 540 for Achada do Teixeira, the number printed on IFCN's trailhead panels) and a Funchal taxi (Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476, online booking at taxismadeira.pt; AITRAM +351 291 765 760, both listed by Visit Madeira). Ask each for a quote for the whole trip and take the better one.
  • Own or hire car: the upper car park at the summit costs €4.00 per hour, up to €20 a day (first 30 minutes free; about 70 spaces). The lower car park on the access road costs €2 per hour, up to €5 a day (IFCN price list). Car parks fill up at sunrise, so arrive early. The catch is PR1's one-way rule: your car stays at Areeiro while you finish at Achada do Teixeira, so you need a taxi or transfer back to it.

If you need a ride-hailing app in Madeira, your option is Bolt. Taxis are available from Funchal, Santana and other towns (numbers above).

Do you need a ticket just to watch the sunrise?

The fee is charged for entering the classified trail (Portaria 48/2026, arts. 5–6). The Pico do Areeiro viewpoint is by the car park, outside the trail, so watching the sunrise there should not need a ticket. We haven't found an official statement that says so explicitly, so check with IFCN if in doubt.

Once you walk onto PR1, you need a valid SIMplifica ticket for your slot. Walking an IFCN classified trail without one is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13), and the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500.

The one-way finish: plan the way back first

Official IFCN list: Pico do Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija viewpoint: both ways. Full trail to Pico Ruivo: one way only, ending at Achada do Teixeira.

Since the full route reopened on 1 May 2026, PR1 beyond Pedra Rija is one-way: Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo, then down PR1.2 to Achada do Teixeira. No bus serves Achada do Teixeira, and IFCN says hikers are responsible for their own transport (IFCN FAQ 5.5). So there are two halves to plan: getting up for sunrise, and getting back from Achada around midday. When only Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija is open (see the card above), it's an out-and-back and the bus works both ways.

Sunrise transport: questions

Is there a bus to Pico do Areeiro for sunrise?

Yes. Horários do Funchal's “Pico do Areeiro” bus leaves the Teleférico stop in Funchal at 06:00 and reaches the summit at 06:45, before sunrise every day of the year. It costs €3.00 per trip, paid on board, and runs daily except 25 December. Seats can be reserved by phone on 291 705 555, 7 to 2 working days ahead.

How much is a PR1 sunrise transfer?

Shared and private hiker transfers run to Pico do Areeiro for sunrise; prices vary, so ask for a quote when you book. The PR1 ticket is usually extra: €10.50 on SIMplifica, or €7 if the operator books it. Taxis charge by the meter at the official tariff, with no fixed price per route.

Do I need a SIMplifica ticket just to watch the sunrise at Pico do Areeiro?

The fee applies to entering the classified trail, and the viewpoint is by the car park, outside it, so watching the sunrise there should not need a ticket. We found no explicit official statement, so check with IFCN if unsure. Walking onto PR1 needs a valid ticket for your slot.

Which PR1 slot should I book for sunrise?

Slots at 07:00 and 07:30 were added in June 2026 for sunrise demand. From mid-May to late July the sun rises around 07:00, so the 07:00 slot fits. The rest of the year sunrise is later (up to about 08:10), so pick a slot that starts after sunrise if you want to watch it from the viewpoint first.

How do I get back from Achada do Teixeira after PR1?

No bus serves Achada do Teixeira. Book a transfer that collects you there, or arrange a taxi in advance (Táxis Santana +351 291 572 540, as printed on IFCN panels). Taxis charge by the meter at the official tariff, with no fixed price per route, so ask for a quote when you book.
