How to get back from Pico Ruivo and Achada do Teixeira without a car

PR1 runs one-way since the 1 May 2026 reopening, so you finish at Achada do Teixeira, a car park above Santana with no bus, far from where you started, and you can't walk PR1 back. Without a car, book a taxi to collect you there at a set time (Táxis Santana, +351 291 572 540, is the nearest rank; Funchal taxis also do the trip), take a shared or private transfer (easiest booked through your hotel reception), or skip the traverse and walk PR1.2 up and back from Achada.

The public bus to Pico do Areeiro (the start only)

Horários do Funchal runs a "Pico do Areeiro" service every day except 25 December. It serves the start of PR1, not Achada do Teixeira, so it solves half the problem.

Summer timetable (to 24 Oct 2026)MorningAfternoon
Leaves Funchal, Teleférico terminus (stop 4A)06:0013:30
Arrives Pico do Areeiro (Radar roundabout)06:4514:15
Leaves Pico do Areeiro for Funchal12:1519:00 (18:00 from 25 Oct)
  • Fare €3.00 per trip, paid on board; other transport tickets aren't accepted. 31 seats.
  • Seat reservations by phone only: 291 705 555, 7 to 2 working days before the trip (Mon–Thu 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–18:00, Fri 08:30–12:30 and 14:00–17:00). Be at the stop 15 minutes before departure.
  • Summit shuttle between the CAM Florestal das Serras de Santo António stop, the Pico do Areeiro car park and the Radar roundabout: every 15 minutes, 06:40–12:15 and 14:10–19:00 (18:00 in winter), €1.20, runs with at least 4 passengers.

The 06:45 arrival is before sunrise all year (only just, in June and July). It works for a sunrise start, or for a Pedra Rija there-and-back with the 12:15 bus down. For the full traverse you still need someone to collect you at Achada. More in sunrise transport to Pico do Areeiro.

Taxis from Achada do Teixeira

Taxis don't wait at Achada do Teixeira. Book one for a set pick-up time before you start, or call from the trail end if you have signal. Save the number first.

Taxi rankPhone
Táxis Santana (the rank nearest Achada do Teixeira)+351 291 572 540
Táxis Funchal+351 291 764 476

Numbers as printed on IFCN's trailhead panels (the PR1.1 and PR1.2 panels for Santana). Whether each line is staffed today isn't verified.

What it costs: taxis charge by the meter at the official regulated tariff, set by the Region's transport authority (IMT-RAM) with the taxi associations and in force since 1 January 2026. There is no fixed price per route; nights, weekends and holidays cost more. Ask for a quote when you book.

Compare two quotes: call the rank nearest the pick-up (Táxis Santana +351 291 572 540 for Achada do Teixeira) and a Funchal taxi (Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476, online booking at taxismadeira.pt; AITRAM +351 291 765 760, both listed by Visit Madeira). Ask each for a quote for the whole trip and take the better one.

If you need a ride-hailing app in Madeira, your option is Bolt. Taxis are available from Funchal, Santana and other towns (numbers above).

Cheaper: a taxi down to Santana, then the bus

No bus reaches Achada do Teixeira, but Santana, down the road, has CAM buses to Funchal. Book a taxi from Achada do Teixeira to Santana only (a much shorter ride than all the way to Funchal; ask the fare when you book) and take the bus from there.

Santana → Funchal. Monday to Friday: 11:00 (route 801, in Funchal 12:30, or route 885, 12:40), 12:30 (801, 14:25) and 17:25 (860, 19:00). Saturdays: 12:00 (801, 13:30), 13:15 (860, 14:20) and 17:28 (860, 19:20). Sundays and holidays: 15:30 (801, 16:50) and 17:28 (860, 19:20).

Times as printed on CAM's Santana network timetable; times at intermediate stops are approximate and can change with traffic or bad weather. No buses on 25 December. Check before you travel with CAM: info@cam-madeira.pt, +351 291 201 150.

Transfers and other options

Shared hiker transfer 1–2 people

A minibus drops you at Pico do Areeiro (often before sunrise) and collects you at Achada do Teixeira at a set time. Prices vary, so ask for a quote. The trail ticket is usually extra: €10.50 on SIMplifica, or €7 when an operator with an IFCN protocol books it. Examples, all listed by Visit Madeira: Beyond Madeira, Doit Madeira, GetToHikes. The easiest way to book is through your hotel reception. Book the day before at the latest; sunrise pick-ups fill up.

Private transfer Groups

Your own vehicle, drop at Areeiro and pick-up at Achada. One seller, madeira.best, offers it for up to 8 people; ask for a quote. Shared between 4–8 people it can cost less per head than shared seats.

Guided tour with transport Zero logistics

IFCN protocol operators bundle the ticket (€7 on PR1), a guide and both transfers. The most expensive option, but closures and sold-out slots become their problem.

Two cars Groups

Leave one car at Achada do Teixeira and drive the other to Pico do Areeiro. It costs an hour of shuttling plus Areeiro parking (€4/h in the upper car park).

Walking PR1 back from Pico Ruivo to Pico do Areeiro is not permitted: beyond Pedra Rija the route is one-way. Rangers are on the trail.

Before you go

  • Check today's PR1 status. It comes from IFCN's official warnings list. Your SIMplifica slot has rules about rescheduling.
  • Screenshot your ticket. Signal on the ridge is unreliable and rangers do check.
  • Set the pick-up time with margin: Visit Madeira gives about 3 hours for Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo, then allow about an hour down PR1.2 to Achada do Teixeira, more with photo stops.

Questions

Is there a bus from Achada do Teixeira?

No. No regular bus serves Achada do Teixeira; the nearest bus services are down in Santana. The only public bus in the area is Horários do Funchal's Pico do Areeiro service (€3), which serves the start of PR1, not the end. Arrange a taxi or transfer to collect you.

How much is a taxi from Achada do Teixeira to Pico do Areeiro or Funchal?

There is no fixed price per route. Taxis charge by the meter at the official tariff set by the Region's transport authority (IMT-RAM) with the taxi associations, in force since 1 January 2026; nights, weekends and holidays cost more. Ask for a quote when you book, and book ahead for a set pick-up time; Táxis Santana is on +351 291 572 540 (the number on IFCN's trailhead panels).

Is there a bus to Pico do Areeiro?

Yes. Horários do Funchal's Pico do Areeiro service leaves Funchal (Teleférico terminus) at 06:00 and 13:30 every day except 25 December, arriving at 06:45 and 14:15, and comes back down at 12:15 and 19:00 (18:00 in winter). The fare is €3.00, paid on board; seats can be reserved on 291 705 555.

Can I walk back from Pico Ruivo to Pico do Areeiro?

No. Since the 1 May 2026 reopening the full route is one-way, Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo. Only the 1.2 km Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija section is two-way. From Pico Ruivo you go down PR1.2 to Achada do Teixeira.

How much does a shared hiker transfer cost?

Prices vary by company and group size, so ask for a quote when you book. The trail ticket is usually extra: €10.50 on SIMplifica, or €7 if an operator with an IFCN protocol books it.
