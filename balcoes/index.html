Book it. PR11 on SIMplifica

Short and easy — but you still need to book, and being family-friendly it fills up.

  1. Book your €4.50 slot on SIMplifica in advance, in a 30-minute window. Under-12s are free but must be on the booking.
  2. Aim for a clear day. The viewpoint's whole point is the peaks — Pico do Areeiro, Pico das Torres and Pico Ruivo — so pick a bright morning if you can.
PR11 is €4.50 per person on SIMplifica; under-12s and residents free but counted on the reservation. Even the easy walks are booking-only now — check the live status above before you go.

Getting there. Ribeiro Frio

The walk starts at Ribeiro Frio, a small forest village on the ER103 — about 40 minutes from Funchal.

  • Park in Ribeiro Frio village (roadside; it's small and can be busy). There's a café and a trout farm for afterwards.
  • The path follows the Levada da Serra do Faial — wide, level and shaded through laurel forest — a gentle 1.5 km to the Balcões miradouro (viewpoint).
  • It's the easy win: nearly flat, short, and doable with kids or on a day you don't want a big climb.

PR11 by bus from Funchal (CAM)

Getting there. Ribeiro Frio, where the walk starts and ends, is a stop on CAM's Santana-line buses from Funchal. Monday to Friday: 08:10 (route 801, at Ribeiro Frio about 08:50), 09:00 (801, 09:41), 10:00 (801, 10:41) and 13:45 (802, 14:31). Saturdays: 07:30 (850, 08:12), 10:00 (801, 10:42) and 13:30 (850, 14:13). Sundays and holidays: 07:30 (850, 08:12) and 10:30 (801, 11:15).

Getting back. Catch the bus to Funchal at the same stop. Monday to Friday: 06:56 (885, in Funchal 07:40), 11:40 (801, in Funchal 12:30), 13:15 in school term or 13:45 in school holidays (801, 14:25) and 18:12 (860, 19:00). Saturdays: 07:25 (860, in Funchal 08:10) and 18:30 (860, 19:20). Sundays and holidays: 08:50 (860, in Funchal 09:35) and 18:30 (860, 19:20). After the morning bus, 18:30 is the only one back.

Closed or full? Your options

  • Reschedule via the SIMplifica call centre before your date — date/time freely, another trail only if yours was officially closed.
  • Other gentle options: check the live board — or pair it with the summit via Pico Ruivo on a clear day.

Three things people find out too late

  • You still have to book. It's the easiest walk on the island, but it's booking-only on SIMplifica like the rest.
  • The view needs a clear day. In cloud the miradouro is just white — check the forecast for the peaks, and the live status here.
  • It's popular because it's easy. Family groups fill the slots, especially midday — book ahead and go early for a quiet path.

Questions people ask

Is the Balcões trail open today?

Today's status is shown live at the top of this page, taken from the official IFCN warnings list, which carries IFCN's own “updated” date. Access is €4.50 through the SIMplifica portal; children under 12 are free but must be on the booking.

Is the Balcões walk easy and good for kids?

Yes — it is Madeira's easiest levada walk: about 1.5 km each way, nearly flat (870–880 m), on a wide levada path through laurel forest to a viewpoint. It suits families and anyone wanting a short, gentle walk.

Do you need to book the Balcões trail?

Yes. Even though it is short and easy, PR11 requires an advance booking and €4.50 payment through SIMplifica, in a 30-minute entry slot. Under-12s are free but must be on the booking.

Where do you park for the Balcões?

At Ribeiro Frio on the ER103, about 40 minutes from Funchal. The trail starts by the road; the village has a café and a trout farm for afterwards.
