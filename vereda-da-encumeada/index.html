PR1.3: Is the Vereda da Encumeada open today?

Vereda da Encumeada (PR1.3) is OPEN on IFCN's official trail-warnings list. It is a paid, booking-only PR trail; access is €4.50 through the SIMplifica portal. See all Madeira trails · PR1.

Do you need to book the Vereda da Encumeada?

Yes. PR1.3 is booking-only: once it shows open, book your €4.50 slot on SIMplifica in advance. Under-12s and residents are free but must still be named on the booking. PR1.3 is paid on its own separate booking and not included in the multi-day passes — check the live status above before you pay.

Where does the Vereda da Encumeada start and finish?

The Vereda da Encumeada runs from Casa de Abrigo do Pico Ruivo to Encumeada. It's a point-to-point walk, so it doesn't finish where you parked — sort your return first (two cars, a pre-booked taxi, or a guided walk with transfers).

It's a long mountain drive from the coast, with no shop or reliable signal at most trailheads. Bring water, warm and waterproof layers, and a torch — the weather turns fast up high.

Where to park for PR1.3?

There is no road at the start, by Pico Ruivo: park at Achada do Teixeira and walk up PR1.2, or at Encumeada, where the trail ends. We found no official parking information for either end.

PR1.3 by bus

Getting there. The walk starts at the Pico Ruivo shelter, which no bus or road reaches: you get there on foot by PR1 or PR1.2.

Getting back. The walk ends at Encumeada, which has a bus back to Funchal. Rodoeste line 6, Encumeada → Funchal. Every day: 16:00 → 17:05. It is the only bus of the day from there, so don't miss it.

Times as printed on the operators' timetables published on SIGA, the Region's public transport site; times at intermediate stops are approximate. No buses on 25 December. Check before you travel.

What if PR1.3 is closed or fully booked?

If IFCN closes PR1.3 (weather, landslip, works) or you can't get a slot: reschedule via the SIMplifica call centre before your date — you can move date/time freely, and switch trail only if yours was officially closed. For open alternatives, check the live board.

Before you go

  • Booking-only. Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500.
  • It doesn't loop back. You finish somewhere else — fix your return before you set off.
  • Check the live badge the morning you go. Mountain trails close fast for weather, rockfall or works.
