Two days in Madeira: a timed itinerary that fits

Day one by taxi to the island's viewpoints, day two on foot and by cable car in Funchal and Monte — the whole top 5, the Botanical Garden, and honest 2026 prices.

What can you do in Madeira in two days?

Everything the top 5 asks for, without rushing: on day one a taxi loop to the Eira do Serrado viewpoint and Curral das Freiras, the Cabo Girão skywalk and Câmara de Lobos; on day two Funchal on foot — the Mercado and the Old Town — then the cable car to Monte, the Monte Palace garden and the Botanical Garden. It is the two plans of our one-day guide done properly, one per day.

Day 1 — Curral das Freiras, Cabo Girão and Câmara de Lobos: what times?

A taxi loop of about 56 km, roughly 6 hours door to door. Agree the price for this exact round trip when you book (drivers quoted us about €160 for a 6-hour day in October 2026 — indicative, not a tariff): Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476 (taxismadeira.pt) or AITRAM +351 291 765 760.

TimeStop
09:00Taxi pickup in Funchal
09:30Eira do Serrado viewpoint over the Curral das Freiras valley
10:15Curral das Freiras town (2.6 km below the viewpoint)
11:15Drive across to the south coast (about 35 min)
12:00Cabo Girão skywalk — 20–30 minutes (€5 over-12s, open 08:00–20:00)
12:45Câmara de Lobos: lunch, then the old town and the pier
14:45Back to Funchal (about 15 min)
15:15Mercado dos Lavradores (weekdays to 19:00)

On a Saturday see the Mercado first thing — it closes at 14:00; on a Sunday it is closed, so move it to day two. Road distances from OpenStreetMap; these are mountain roads, so times are without traffic.

Day 2 — Funchal, Monte and the Botanical Garden: what times?

On foot and by cable car — no taxi needed. The Botanical Garden leg costs about €21 per adult (€11 cable car one way + €10 entry), so skip it if gardens aren't your thing and take the toboggans instead (below).

TimeStop
09:00Old Town stroll — and the Mercado, if day one was a weekend
10:00Cable car up to Monte (08:45–17:45; €16 one way, €22 return)
10:30Monte church
11:00Monte Palace garden, about 2 hours (€18, up to 14 free; to 18:00 in winter, 19:00 April–September)
13:00Light lunch
14:00Monte–Botanical Garden cable car (09:00–17:00; €11 one way) across the valley
14:15Botanical Garden (€10 over-12s, €3 ages 6–12, under-6 free; open to 17:30)
16:30Taxi down to town — they wait at the garden's exits, about €12 to the cable-car station — or the bus

The alternative without the Botanical Garden: after Monte Palace, start the descent on the Carreiros do Monte wicker toboggans (Monday–Saturday 09:00–18:00) and continue by bus or taxi.

How much do two days in Madeira cost?

Per adult, at 2026 prices: day one is the taxi loop — about €160 for the car for 6 hours (indicative; split it between everyone in the taxi) plus €5 for Cabo Girão. Day two: €16 cable car up to Monte, €18 Monte Palace, then either the Botanical Garden leg — €11 cable + €10 entry, plus about €12 for the taxi down to the cable-car station (they wait at the garden's exits), or €26 with the garden cable there and back or the toboggan ride instead (price on the Carreiros do Monte site). The Mercado, the Old Town, the viewpoints and both towns on day one cost nothing beyond the taxi.

Should I rent a car for two days in Madeira?

For these two days, no. Parking is limited at most of the day-one stops, and day two is entirely on foot and cable car — the car would sit in a paid Funchal garage all day. Rent a car only if you are adding a third day of your own route.

Can I swap in a levada or a peak?

Yes — the short mountain taste that fits this plan is the out-and-back from Pico do Areeiro to the Pedra Rija viewpoint on PR1 (1.2 km each way, €4.50, booked online on SIMplifica — see how to book). Put it at the start of day one and shift the loop an hour later, and check the summit first: Pico do Areeiro weather and webcams.

From a cruise ship

Two port days, or an overnight call, split exactly this way: the taxi loop on the first day, Funchal and Monte on the second. Agree each return time with the driver when you book and keep at least an hour's margin before all-aboard. What's open in town today — events, lidos, last buses — is on Funchal today.
