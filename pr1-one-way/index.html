Is the one-way section open today?

IFCN closes the Pedra Rija – Pico Ruivo section on some days for scheduled works (it did on 14–17 and 21–22 September 2026), while Areeiro ↔ Pedra Rija stays open both ways.   • The full route starts at Pico do Areeiro, not at the Pedra Rija gate: enter in the Areeiro area at your slot time (IFCN FAQ 5.2).
  • There is no way back along PR1 once you pass Pedra Rija: Visit Madeira describes the full route as one-way "with no possibility of returning along the same path".
  • PR1 follows the west variant through the tunnels. The east variant over Pico das Torres is listed as closed (IFCN trail list).
  • Want Pico Ruivo without the one-way logistics? PR1.2 from Achada do Teixeira is a separate trail that IFCN lists as 2.8 km plus 2.8 km back.

Why is it one-way?

The official texts state the rule but not the reason. IFCN's FAQ (5.3) only says that from Pedra Rija "the trail operates on a one-way basis, in the direction Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo", and the Regional Government announced it together with the reopening (JM). The section beyond Pedra Rija had been closed since a rockfall caused by the August 2024 wildfire, and repair work carried on after the reopening. Some tour and blog sites say one-way traffic eases crowding on the narrow ridge; that is their explanation, not an official one.

Which ticket: €10.50 or €4.50?

  • Full traverse, Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo: €10.50 on SIMplifica. SIMplifica sells it as "exclusivamente unidirecional" (one-way only), and it includes PR1.1 and PR1.2 for the way down.
  • Areeiro – Pedra Rija and back: €4.50. SIMplifica's own wording: two-way to the Pedra Rija viewpoint at km 1.2, and it "does not give access to Pico Ruivo".
  • Through an IFCN protocol operator, the full PR1 costs €7 (Portaria 48/2026).
  • No upgrades: a €4.50 ticket can't be converted. To go on to Pico Ruivo you need a new €10.50 ticket, and the €4.50 isn't refunded (IFCN FAQ 5.7–5.8).
  • Children aged 12 and under, residents and people with a disability of 60% or more are exempt, but must still be on the booking. PR1 is not part of the day or multi-day rates.

Plan the way back before you book

Because PR1 is one-way, you finish at Achada do Teixeira, far from your start. No bus serves Achada do Teixeira, and IFCN says hikers are responsible for their own transport (IFCN FAQ 5.5). If you drove to Areeiro, your car stays there.

  • Shared hiker transfer that drops you at Areeiro and collects you at Achada: prices vary, so ask for a quote.
  • Taxi, booked in advance: taxis don't wait at Achada. Taxis charge by the meter at the official tariff set by the Region's transport authority (IMT-RAM) with the taxi associations (in force since 1 January 2026); there is no fixed price per route, and nights, weekends and holidays cost more. Call the rank nearest the pick-up (Táxis Santana +351 291 572 540, as printed on IFCN's trailhead panels) and a Funchal taxi (Táxis Madeira +351 291 764 476, online booking at taxismadeira.pt; AITRAM +351 291 765 760, both listed by Visit Madeira). Ask each for a quote for the whole trip and take the better one.

PR1 one-way: questions

Is PR1 Pico do Areeiro one-way?

Yes. Since the full route reopened on 1 May 2026, PR1 is one-way from the Pedra Rija viewpoint to Pico Ruivo, in the direction Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo. Only the first 1.2 km, between Pico do Areeiro and Pedra Rija, is still two-way. You finish by going down from Pico Ruivo to Achada do Teixeira (PR1.2) or to Ilha (PR1.1).

Can I hike PR1 from Pico Ruivo to Pico do Areeiro?

No. IFCN only allows the section between Pedra Rija and Pico Ruivo in the direction of Pico Ruivo, so you can't start at Pico Ruivo or Achada do Teixeira and walk to Pico do Areeiro, and you can't walk the whole route there and back.

Can I walk from Pico do Areeiro to Pedra Rija and back?

Yes. Pico do Areeiro – Pedra Rija viewpoint (1.2 km each way) is still two-way. It has its own €4.50 SIMplifica ticket, which does not let you continue to Pico Ruivo.

Since when has PR1 been one-way?

Since the full reopening on 1 May 2026. The one-way section first opened Friday to Sunday only, because of surface repairs, and has been open every day since 26 June 2026. IFCN still closes it on some days for works, so check the live status before you go.

Why is PR1 one-way?

IFCN and the Regional Government state the rule but have not published a reason in the texts we checked. The rule came with the reopening after repairs to the rockfall caused by the August 2024 wildfire. The crowding explanation found on some tour sites is not an official one.

How do I get back after hiking PR1 one-way?

No bus serves Achada do Teixeira, and IFCN does not arrange the return. Book a transfer that collects you at Achada do Teixeira, or arrange a taxi in advance (Táxis Santana +351 291 572 540, as printed on IFCN panels). Taxis charge by the meter at the official tariff, with no fixed price per route, so ask for a quote when you book.
