Levadinho · Madeira trail answers

Which levadas need a torch or headlamp?

Every official PR trail with tunnels, from IFCN's trailhead panels and Visit Madeira, with the live status of each.

The short answer: bring a torch or headlamp on PR16 Fajã do Rodrigues (the last tunnel is about 1 km, 20–25 minutes), PR9 Caldeirão Verde (four tunnels), PR6.6 Túnel do Cavalo (about 800 m), PR17 Pináculo e Folhadal, PR10 Levada do Furado, PR28 Rocha Vermelha, PR6.4 Levada Velha do Rabaçal and PR1 Areeiro–Ruivo: IFCN tells you to on each one. PR18 Levada do Rei has only a very short tunnel, and the standard PR6 25 Fontes route has none.

Each trail below shows its status from the official IFCN list, with IFCN's own “updated” date.

What to bring

  • A headlamp rather than your phone. It keeps both hands free on a wet, uneven floor, and your phone battery stays for maps and emergencies (112). IFCN's panels simply say “carry a torch”.
  • One light per person, plus spare batteries or a charged power bank. PR16's last tunnel alone takes 20–25 minutes.
  • A rain jacket. Tunnels can drip: guides describe the Túnel do Cavalo as long, wet and unlit.
  • Shoes with grip. IFCN warns that paths on these trails can be slippery.
  • Tall? Expect to stoop in low sections (reported on PR16).

Tunnels people mention that aren't on the PR route

  • The “800 m tunnel” at 25 Fontes is the Túnel do Cavalo on PR6.6, its own trail. The standard PR6 route (Rabaçal to 25 Fontes, back by the Bypass) has no tunnel.
  • The 1.6–1.8 km “Rocha Vermelha tunnel” is on Calheta council's access route from Malhada (about 1.7 km, says the council), not on official PR28, which starts at Fanal.
  • The long tunnel on “Levada do Moinho” walks is on the unofficial Levada Nova extension. Official PR7 shows no tunnel on its IFCN panel or its Visit Madeira page.
  • The “heather tunnels” on PR6.2 and the plant “tunnels” on PR18 are arches of vegetation, not rock tunnels.
  • On Porto Santo, PR3 Levada do Pico do Castelo has a tunnel with a slippery floor; IFCN says to bring a flashlight.

Questions

Which levadas need a torch?

IFCN's trailhead panels say to carry a torch on PR9 Caldeirão Verde, PR16 Fajã do Rodrigues, PR17 Pináculo e Folhadal, PR10 Levada do Furado, PR6.6 Túnel do Cavalo, PR6.4 Levada Velha do Rabaçal and PR28 Rocha Vermelha. IFCN's PR1 leaflet says the same for Pico do Areeiro to Pico Ruivo.

Is a phone light enough in the tunnels?

IFCN doesn't say; its panels just say to carry a torch. A headlamp is the practical choice: your hands stay free on a wet, uneven floor and your phone battery stays for navigation and emergencies. PR16's last tunnel takes 20–25 minutes to walk.

Does Levada das 25 Fontes have a tunnel?

Not on the standard PR6 route from Rabaçal. The 800 m tunnel people mention is the Túnel do Cavalo, on the separate trail PR6.6. You need a torch for that one.
