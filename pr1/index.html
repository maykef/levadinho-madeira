Is PR1 one-way? Yes, beyond Pedra Rija

  • Pico do Areeiro → Pico Ruivo (full route): one-way only. In force since the full reopening on 1 May 2026 (Fridays to Sundays at first, every day since 26 June 2026). You can't walk it from Pico Ruivo back to Pico do Areeiro.
  • Pico do Areeiro ↔ Miradouro da Pedra Rija (1.2 km each way): two-way. A there-and-back from the Areeiro car park.
  • Two different SIMplifica tickets: €4.50 for Areeiro–Pedra Rija only, €10.50 for the full route (the PR1.1, PR1.2 and PR1.3 exits are included). Booked through an IFCN protocol operator: €3 / €7. Ages 12 and under go free but must be on the booking.
  • You finish at Achada do Teixeira, down PR1.2 from Pico Ruivo (about an hour). No bus goes there, so plan the ride back first: getting back without a car · sunrise transport to Pico do Areeiro.
Official sources disagree right now. IFCN's warnings list still lists the one-way Pedra Rija → Pico Ruivo section as closed after the September walkway works, while Visit Madeira shows PR1 open. Before you go, check whether SIMplifica sells full-route (€10.50) slots for your date.

Sold out? What can I do if PR1 slots are gone on SIMplifica?

PR1 has capacity limits and 30-minute entry slots. Morning windows go first. In order of what actually works:

  1. Try weekday, mid-morning slots. Everyone fights for sunrise. 09:00–10:30 midweek is often available days after dawn slots are gone — and you skip the parking chaos.
  2. Re-check the SIMplifica booking page the evening before. Cancellations get released back. Rescheduled bookings free up slots with no notice.
  3. Book through an IFCN protocol operator. Licensed guides hold their own allocation and handle the permit for you (their trail fee is discounted to €7 on PR1). Costs more overall, solves transport too.
  4. Do the summit anyway — from the other side. PR1.2 from Achada do Teixeira reaches Pico Ruivo (1,862 m, the island's highest point) in about 45–60 min each way. Standard €4.50 fee, its own booking, usually far more availability.
PR1 is paid via SIMplifica and not included in the 1/3/7-day multi-day passes. The fee depends on which section is open: €4.50 for the Pico do Areeiro–Pedra Rija section, or €10.50 for the full one-way traverse to Pico Ruivo when open. Ages 12 and under free; parking €4.00/hour. Check the live status above for today.

Sunrise? How to do PR1 at dawn

Sunrise above the clouds at Pico do Areeiro is why so many people on Madeira set a 4 a.m. alarm. The rules confuse everyone, so here's the short version:

  • Do I need a ticket to watch the sunrise at Pico do Areeiro? Most likely not, but check. The fee applies to entering the classified trail, and the viewpoint is by the car park, outside it. We found no explicit official statement, so if in doubt ask IFCN or check SIMplifica. Walking onto PR1 itself always needs a ticket for your slot.
  • Hiking at first light needs the earliest slot: 07:00–07:30. Early 07:00 and 07:30 slots were added in June 2026 because of sunrise demand — before that, entry started at 08:00. Same fee as any other window; there is no sunrise surcharge.
  • Match the slot to the season. Sunrise ranges from about 06:50 in midsummer to 08:05 in midwinter. In summer, watch it from the viewpoint, then start walking at 07:00. In late autumn and winter, the 07:00 slot puts you on the ridge in the dark for sunrise from the trail itself — bring a headlamp.
  • Parking is the real bottleneck. Arrive at least an hour before sunrise: the lots fill, cars line the access road, and police sometimes close it when it saturates. And dress warm — it can be single digits °C at 1,818 m before dawn even in August.

The no-car version: sunrise transfer minibuses pick up in Funchal/Caniço around 05:00–05:30, drop you at the summit before dawn and collect you at Achada do Teixeira after the traverse — solving parking and the one-way return in one go. The trail ticket usually isn't included: book it yourself, or go with an IFCN protocol operator (€7).

The 07:00–07:30 window is the first to sell out — often days ahead in July–August. Book as far ahead as SIMplifica allows and re-check the evening before for released cancellations.

Closed? How do I reschedule my SIMplifica booking?

If IFCN officially closes the trail (weather, rockfall, works), you're entitled to change — but there's a procedure:

  1. Contact the SIMplifica call centre before your scheduled date. Not IFCN — they don't manage the portal.
  2. You can move to a different date or time on the same trail freely.
  3. You can switch to a different trail only if yours was officially closed by the authorities. A change of plans on your side doesn't qualify.

Open alternatives that don't feel like a consolation prize:

  • PR1.2 Vereda do Pico Ruivo — the summit without the ridge. Moderate, family-doable.
  • PR9 Levada do Caldeirão Verde — tunnels, waterfalls, green canyon. Bring a headlamp.
  • PR11 Vereda dos Balcões — 3 km, nearly flat, big valley views. The easy win on a foggy summit day.

Three things people find out too late

  • PR1 is one-way now (since the 1 May 2026 reopening): beyond Pedra Rija, Areeiro → Ruivo only. You finish at Achada do Teixeira — plan your ride back before you start.
  • No cash, no kiosk for PR1. Booking is online-only. Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500.
  • Summit weather ≠ Funchal weather. It can be 10–15°C colder at 1,800 m, with fog while the coast is sunny. Check the mountain forecast, not the city one.
