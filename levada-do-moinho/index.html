Do you need to book the Levada do Moinho?

Yes. PR7 is booking-only: once it shows open, book your €4.50 slot on SIMplifica in advance. Under-12s and residents are free but must still be named on the booking. PR7 is paid on its own separate booking and not included in the multi-day passes — check the live status above before you pay.

Where does the Levada do Moinho start and finish?

The Levada do Moinho starts and finishes at Ribeira da Cruz, so you return the way you came — no return transport to arrange.

It's a long mountain drive from the coast, with no shop or reliable signal at most trailheads. Bring water, warm and waterproof layers, and a torch — the weather turns fast up high.

What's the weather at Levada do Moinho (PR7) now?

The status card above shows the latest measured reading from the IPMA Rabaçal station, the regional weather station nearest the trail: temperature, wind and whether it is likely in cloud. Expect the mountains cooler and cloudier than Funchal.

What if PR7 is closed or fully booked?

If IFCN closes PR7 (weather, landslip, works) or you can't get a slot: reschedule via the SIMplifica call centre before your date — you can move date/time freely, and switch trail only if yours was officially closed. For open alternatives, check the live board.

Before you go

  • Booking-only. Walking an IFCN classified trail without a valid SIMplifica ticket is an administrative offence (Portaria 801/2025 art. 10; DLR 24/2022/M art. 13); the law sets fines for individuals of €250–€2,500.
  • It's out-and-back. Turn around with enough time and daylight to walk out the way you came.
  • Check the live badge the morning you go. Mountain trails close fast for weather, rockfall or works.
