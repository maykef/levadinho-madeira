Levadinho · Madeira trail answers

Easiest levada walks without vertigo

Ranked by the official warnings on IFCN's trailhead panels and checked against independent guides. If we found no sourced rating for a trail, it isn't listed.

The short answer: for the least exposure, choose the two purpose-built accessible routes, PR6.8 Levada do Paul II and PR9.1 Caldeirão Verde “um caminho para todos”, or PR11 Vereda dos Balcões, PR13 Fanal and PR6.2 Levada do Alecrim. Be careful with the usual “easy” tips: IFCN's own trailhead panels warn of danger of vertigo on PR18 Levada do Rei and PR14 Levada dos Cedros, even though most guides call them easy. If drops bother you, skip PR1, PR6 25 Fontes, PR8, PR9, PR16, PR17 and PR19. All need a SIMplifica booking; the fee is €4.50 (PR1 €10.50).

Each trail below shows its status from the official IFCN list, with IFCN's own “updated” date.

How we rate exposure

Low: built for accessibility, or no vertigo warning on the IFCN panel plus at least two independent guides calling it easy. Moderate: an official warning that guides play down. High: an official vertigo or cliff warning that guides agree with, or two or more guides rating it high. Trails with no sourced rating are left out.

3. Vereda dos Balcões PR11…

1.5 km (3 km round trip) · 1:30 h · Easy · Fee €4.50

Visit Madeira calls it easy, IFCN's panel has no vertigo warning, and guides describe a “wide, level path” (juliedawnfox). One caveat: the Balcões viewpoint at the end looks over big drops, though it's fenced (hikingmadeira).

Low exposure

4. Vereda do Fanal PR13…

10.8 km · 4 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50

No vertigo warning on IFCN's panel; its warning is fog, which makes it easy to lose your way. Guides rate it among the gentlest (madeirahiking.org, madeiralovers). It's long, but you can turn back whenever you like.

Low exposureFog: stay on the trail

5. Levada do Alecrim PR6.2…

3.5 km (7 km round trip) · 2:30 h · Easy · Fee €4.50

Two independent guides say it has no serious exposure (hikingmadeira; waldspaziergang.org, as the “Rosmarin-Levada”). There's no IFCN panel for it. The “tunnels” Visit Madeira mentions are arches of heather, not rock tunnels.

Low exposure

2. Called easy, but with an official warning

These often appear on “no vertigo” lists. They're mostly gentle, but IFCN's panels disagree with that label in places, so go knowing that.

Levada do Rei PR18…

5.3 km (10.6 km round trip) · 3:30 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50

Most guides call it one of the easiest levadas: “no steep drop-offs… no narrow ledges” (hikingmadeira). IFCN's own trailhead panel still says: danger of vertigo, path may be slippery. Our reading: mostly wide and gentle, but not free of drops everywhere.

IFCN vertigo warningShort tunnel, no torch needed

Levada dos Cedros PR14…

7.2 km · 3 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50

madeirahiking.org rates it “very low exposure” and madeiralovers calls it easy. But IFCN's panel says danger of vertigo, and Visit Madeira describes passing “the steep slope of the right bank of the Ribeira da Janela”.

IFCN vertigo warningSlippery in parts

Levada do Furado PR10…

11 km · 5 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50

madeirahiking.org puts it in its low-exposure group but mentions “some narrow levada sections with drops”. IFCN's panel says danger of vertigo, and that there are tunnels, so carry a torch.

IFCN vertigo warningTunnels: bring a torch

Levada da Rocha Vermelha PR28…

10.8 km · 4 h · Fee €4.50

IFCN's panel warns of “technical and physical particularities”: make sure it suits you. The famous long tunnel is on Calheta council's access route from Malhada, which the council describes as exposed, without protective walls and liable to cause vertigo.

3. Trails with drops: skip these if you have vertigo

  • PR1 Vereda do Areeiro …
    6.1 km · 3 h · Moderate · Fee €10.50
    Narrow ridge, steep slopes and a carved rock staircase around Pico das Torres; named vertigo-prone by hikingmadeira and madeirahiking.org.
  • PR6 Levada das 25 Fontes …
    4.3 km (8.6 km round trip) · 3 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50
    IFCN's panel: “PR 6 can cause vertigo”. Guides mention narrow ledges and “hair raising drops”.
  • PR8 Vereda da Ponta de São Lourenço …
    4 km · 2:30 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50
    IFCN's panel: don't go near the cliff face, the ground is unstable in many places. “Many unprotected edges” (hikingmadeira).
  • PR9 Levada do Caldeirão Verde …
    8.7 km (17.4 km round trip) · 6:30 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50
    IFCN's panel: danger of vertigo. Guides mention narrow ledges and dark tunnels.
  • PR16 Levada Fajã do Rodrigues …
    3.9 km (7.8 km round trip) · 3:30 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50
    IFCN's panel: danger of vertigo. A narrow levada path with drops, plus long tunnels.
  • PR17 Caminho do Pináculo e Folhadal …
    15 km · 6:30 h · Difficult · Fee €4.50
    No vertigo line on the IFCN panel, but guides say “not for vertigo sufferers” (madeirahiking.org) and “not for the faint-hearted” (Visit Madeira blog).
  • PR19 Caminho Real do Paul do Mar …
    1.8 km · 1:20 h · Moderate · Fee €4.50
    IFCN's panel: danger of vertigo, its only warning.

Not rated here

Visit Madeira calls PR6.1 Levada do Risco and PR6.3 Lagoa do Vento easy, but we found no sourced exposure rating, so they aren't ranked. Levada da Serra do Faial and Levada do Castelejo appear on some “no vertigo” lists, but they aren't official PR trails, so IFCN doesn't maintain them.

Worried about tunnels rather than heights? On PR16 the tunnels are low enough that you have to stoop (sayyestomadeira). See which levadas need a torch.

Questions

Which levada is easiest if I'm afraid of heights?

The accessible routes PR6.8 Levada do Paul II and PR9.1 Caldeirão Verde “um caminho para todos” are built for everyone. Of the regular trails, PR11 Vereda dos Balcões, PR13 Fanal and PR6.2 Levada do Alecrim have the least exposure, with no vertigo warning on IFCN's panels. The Balcões viewpoint itself looks over big drops, but it is fenced.

Is Levada do Rei OK if I get vertigo?

Most guides call Levada do Rei (PR18) one of the easiest levadas, with wide paths. But IFCN's official trailhead panel warns of danger of vertigo and a slippery path, so expect some exposed spots. The same goes for Levada dos Cedros (PR14).

Which levadas should I avoid if I have vertigo?

PR1 Pico do Areeiro to Pico Ruivo, PR6 25 Fontes, PR8 Ponta de São Lourenço, PR9 Caldeirão Verde, PR16 Fajã do Rodrigues, PR17 Pináculo e Folhadal and PR19 Caminho Real do Paul do Mar. Each has an official vertigo or cliff warning or is rated high exposure by guides.
